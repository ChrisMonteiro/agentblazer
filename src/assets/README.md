# AgentBlazer Club Assets

Drop your club photos, videos, and custom logos into these directories:

- `/src/assets/logo/`: Place your high-res `AgentBlazer_Logo.jpg`, `logo.png` or `agentblazer-logo.svg`
- `/src/assets/members/`: Place individual member portraits (e.g. `aditya.jpg`, `sneha.jpg`)
- `/src/assets/events/`: Place event photos from hackathons, workshops, and symposiums
- `/src/assets/gallery/`: Place candid campus moments, trophy ceremonies, and team photos
- `/src/assets/videos/`: Place club promo mp4s or aftermovie clips

To update any image path or data, open `/src/data/clubData.ts` and update the respective item!
All components automatically adapt to new images, videos, names, and links.
